How to read the result

The anomaly model screens for patterns outside its training distribution. K-Means assigns the profile to one of three synthetic clusters. The explanation panel ranks simple feature deviations from demo reference centers. These outputs are descriptive model signals, not causal explanations.

ImportantNo medical probability, diagnosis, treatment suggestion, or real astronaut assessment is produced.
